# Sofka Brand Manual Reference — Official Guidelines 2024

> Source: Sofka_ Brand Guidelines 2024 (confidential)

## 1. Strategy & Positioning

**Positioning Statement:** For businesses seeking to stay current in the digital world, Sofka is
the technology ally that offers tailored experiences and solutions for sustainable and scalable
products over time, thanks to its proximity and focus on continuous improvement.

**Tagline:** Your technology companion.

**Claim:** Better happens every day.

**Claim Variants:**
- Better digital experiences. Every day.
- Better human experiences. Every day.
- Better work experiences. Every day.

**Manifesto Key Lines:**
- "Every moment we share is an opportunity to create and deliver the highest quality technological experiences."
- "Every step we take paves the way to excellence in innovation and service."
- "Every person on this team has an impact on our results."
- "Every client inspires us to do our best to improve the experience through their digital journey."

**Mission:** To transform people's lives, being the most trusted technology partner.

## 2. Logo

The Sofka logo is the word "sofka" in bold lowercase sans-serif with an orange underscore character: **sofka_**

### Usage Rules

1. **Color version** on light or white backgrounds. **Inverted version** (white text, orange underscore) on dark backgrounds.
2. **Clearspace:** Minimum 1x the height of the underscore symbol on all four sides.
3. **Minimum size:** Digital: 72px wide / 20px tall. Print: 1" (25.4mm) wide / 0.22" (5.5mm) tall.
4. **Corner placement:** Position the logo in a corner on a grid for digital and print communications.
5. **Co-branding:** Separate by 2x underscore height. Partner logo height must not exceed Sofka logo height.

### Things to Avoid

- Backgrounds with insufficient contrast
- Logo over busy backgrounds
- Compressing or stretching
- Adding shadows or gradients
- Altering the underscore symbol
- Adding anything to the symbol

### Presentation Color Strategies

**Dark theme (recommended for impact):** Black `#000000` or very dark backgrounds with white text and Sofka.Orange accents. This is the dominant style in the official brand guidelines and corporate presentation template.

**Light theme:** Sofka.Light `#EFEAE4` background with black text and Sofka.Orange accents. Used for content-heavy slides.

**Orange accent sections:** Sofka.Orange `#FF7E08` as background with black text for high-impact statement slides or CTA banners.

**Mixed layout:** Combine dark and orange areas within a single slide using geometric blocks (rounded rectangles, circles) to create dynamic compositions.

## 4. Typography

### Primary: Clash Grotesk

Used for ALL headlines across all communications. Features fine details, moderate contrast, and slightly unusual anatomy. It serves as the loud, proud hero typeface for the brand identity.

- **Weights:** Regular, Medium, SemiBold
- **Usage:** Headlines, section titles, display text, taglines, claims

### Secondary: Inter

Used for body copy when clarity and digestibility are needed over expressiveness.

- **Weights:** Regular, Bold
- **Usage:** Body text, captions, descriptions, fine print

### Typography Rules

1. Use no more than **3 font sizes or weights** in one layout.
2. Use **sentence case** for headlines (not ALL CAPS unless it's an overline/label).
3. Keep copy **flush left** (left-aligned).
4. Text and background must exceed **4.5:1 contrast ratio** for accessibility.
5. Large text (18pt+) and images of large text must have at least **3:1** contrast ratio.

## 5. Grid System

The grid system provides a platform for holistic design across all applications.

### How to Size the Grid

1. Set the logo size to generate the margin.
2. Define the underscore symbol height as the minimum base grid unit.
3. A good starting point: symbol height = **1.5% of (layout width + height)**.
4. Use a **square grid** pattern of squares underlying all layouts for clean lines, balance, and structure.
5. Create columns by dividing the dimension inside the margins into equal-width columns as needed.
6. Leave space between columns of type and images.

## 6. Illustrations

The illustration style uses **simple geometric shapes** on a **strong grid** with **mathematically derived proportions**.

The brand's signature decorative motif is a **radial sun pattern**: orange dashes (`/`, `\`, `|`, `—`) radiating outward from a center point, arranged in a grid-like pattern. This pattern appears:
- As background decoration on photos and dark sections
- As accent elements in corners of compositions
- In various sizes from small (icon-like) to large (full-section background)

Use abstract illustration and visual metaphors inspired by the Kaizen philosophy.

## 7. Photography Style

- Professional shots of people in modern tech workspaces
- Team collaboration, diverse representation (Latin American focus)
- Warm, slightly orange-tinted color grading
- People wearing Sofka-branded purple hoodies and blue polo shirts are common in official photos
- Use navy/dark semi-transparent overlay on background images to ensure text legibility

## 8. Presentation Template Structure

Based on the official corporate presentation template:

### Slide Types

1. **Cover Slide:** Black/dark background, "Better happens every day." claim, person photo on right side with radial sun pattern overlay, sofka_ logo, www.sofka.com.co

2. **Section Divider:** Black background, large claim text, two content photos with rounded corners on right side

3. **Strategy/Mission Slide:** Dark background, large statement text in white with key words highlighted, content photo with orange circle accent in corner, section number and "Your technology companion" footer

4. **Content Slide (dark):** Black background, radial sun pattern as decoration, content photos with rounded corners, body text in columns, section number indicator

5. **Timeline Slide:** Dark background, horizontal timeline with year markers, description cards below each point

6. **Team Slide:** Dark background, circular or rounded-square profile photos, name in white, role text, decorative colored circle (violet, orange) accent

7. **Stats Slide:** Dark background, large numbers in white or orange, descriptive labels below

8. **CTA/Closing Slide:** Photo background with "Better happens every day." overlay, sofka_ logo, dark overlay bar at bottom

### Consistent Elements Across All Slides

- sofka_ logo in top-left or bottom-left corner
- Section number indicator (e.g., "01") in small text
- "Your technology companion" or "sofka_ confidential" in footer
- www.sofka.com.co URL on cover and closing slides
- Rounded corners (12-16px) on all embedded photos and content blocks
- Radial sun pattern as ambient decoration (subtle, not overwhelming)
- Orange circle or semi-circle as geometric accent element
